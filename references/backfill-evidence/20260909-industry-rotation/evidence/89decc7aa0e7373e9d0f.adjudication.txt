Rovo for Microsoft 365: Turn conversations into Jira work Skip to content Atlassian Rovo + Microsoft 365: Turn conversations into completed work, tracked in Jira August 26, 2026 Published In Development Rovo Search your full context, trigger @Jira actions, and delegate work to AI agents in Copilot and Teams. What’s new: Atlassian Rovo is now available as an agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot and Teams , bringing the rich, cross-system context of the Teamwork Graph, directly into your workspace, so you have the full picture to make the right decisions. The Jira Cloud for Microsoft Teams app now understands natural language, powered by Rovo, so you can trigger @Jira workflows and assign work to agents without leaving your chat. Decisions get debated in Teams, documented in Confluence, and tracked in Jira. When these tools operate in silos, context gets lost in chat threads and AI agents lack critical context including existing backlogs, dependencies, and project history. Atlassian Rovo now connects Microsoft 365 directly to the Teamwork Graph – Atlassian’s connected map of your work items and owners in Jira, documentation and decisions in Confluence, and code in GitHub or Bitbucket. If your team already uses the Jira Cloud for Microsoft Teams app , this integration now gives you the context needed to track and execute agent actions directly inside your Microsoft workspace. Starting today, you can tap into that context, whether you’re searching for answers in Copilot or taking action directly in Teams chat. Meet Atlassian Rovo for Microsoft 365 Copilot and Teams With Rovo now available inside Microsoft 365 Copilot and Teams, you have your team’s full thread of work – which Jira work items are in flight, what was decided in Confluence, and where a task is waiting on a dependency – available right where you work. That means your team can go from question to action without leaving the conversation, with full context from Atlassian and your connected apps already in tow. With Rovo in your Microsoft 365 workspace, you can: Search across all your tools at once. Ask Rovo a question and it pulls from Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, and your other connected tools, so you’re not repeating your questions across different surfaces. Get answers that connect the dots. Rovo doesn’t just return documents. It understands which work items block which projects, where decisions were captured, and who owns each next step, giving you a holistic view on the latest, not just a list of documents. Take action without leaving the conversation. With the context to take the next step, let Rovo create Jira work items, update Confluence pages, and move work forward right from Teams. Decisions turn into work completed without anyone switching tools. With Rovo working alongside Microsoft 365 Copilot and Teams, the full context of your work travels with you into every conversation. Customers want AI agents that show up in the flow of their work, as a capability woven into the tools they already use. Bringing Atlassian’s Rovo agent into Microsoft 365 Copilot and Microsoft Teams connects Atlassian’s teamwork data from apps like Jira and Confluence with the collaboration tools of Microsoft 365, so people can get answers, take action, and make decisions forward faster.” — Srini Raghavan, CVP, Copilot and Agents Ecosystem, at Microsoft Natural language actions in Jira Cloud for Microsoft Teams, powered by Rovo More than a million users already rely on the Jira Cloud for Microsoft Teams app for notifications, work item previews, and quick actions. Now, Rovo’s intelligence is built directly into the app, giving it the ability to understand natural language, so work can move from a Teams conversation to a teammate or an AI agent in Jira within seconds. Simply @Jira in any conversation and describe what you need in plain language. Jira uses the thread context to pre-fill the details to create, update, and assign work. Some common scenarios for using @Jira : A status change gets decided in standup: “@Jira, move PROJ-142 to In Review.” Done, without opening Jira. Your team decides to hand a task to a coding agent: “@Jira, assign PROJ-205 to Jira Coding Agent.” The work routes directly to the agent, logged and trackable in Jira. A feature request comes up in a channel: “@Jira, create a work item for adding SSO to the mobile app.” Jira reads the thread, fills in the context, and links it in your channel – with the conversation context connected in Jira. With Jira Cloud for Microsoft Teams, the gap between where decisions happen and where work lives is closed. One connected workflow across Atlassian and Microsoft Work rarely happens in just one tool. Whether you’re managing sprints in Jira, drafting docs in Confluence, or chatting in Teams, Rovo gives you the context to go from conversation to completed work. By connecting the Teamwork Graph directly with Microsoft 365, you no longer have to choose between where your team communicates and where work is tracked. Query your full context with Rovo or trigger @Jira workflows, all without breaking your team’s focus.
