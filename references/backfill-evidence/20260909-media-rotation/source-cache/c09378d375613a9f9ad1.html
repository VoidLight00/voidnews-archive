Updates to Suno Studio

Sep 2, 2026

It’s been a couple of weeks since we launched Studio 2.0, the biggest ever update to our browser-based DAW. We’re constantly working on making improvements, and we’re excited to announce a couple of updates that we hope will make your Studio experience even better. Here’s what’s new:

Chat improvements

We've updated the chat bar performance, and the latest improvements make it aware of bpm, so now it can process tempo changes. It’s also more reliable and more rational with its responses, and you can now undo prompt edits.

Copy and duplicate plugins

You can now move and copy plugins from one track to another by dragging and dropping. (Previously you needed to copy and paste in separate steps.) You can also use cmd-D (Mac) or ctrl-D (PC) to duplicate plugins on the same track, and you can also drag-and-drop while holding the option key to duplicate from one track to another.

Wavetable fidelity

We improved the performance of the wavetable synth, so now you no longer experience aliasing at high frequency. This should result in cleaner and more detailed synth sounds, and smoother frequency shifts.

Overall responsiveness

Audio playback is faster and projects load more quickly, so Studio can keep up with your pace better.

Bug fixes

We fixed an issue where MIDI and MP3 files were missing from some exports; resolved a MIDI issue of stuck notes when pressed and released quickly, and fixed a display bug that caused reversed clips to collapse when resized.

As always, keep the feedback coming; a lot of what's above came straight from you, and it shapes what we build next. Click here for more info on Suno Studio.

Studio 2.0 is here

Aug 13, 2026

We have totally overhauled Suno Studio, our browser-based generative DAW, with features including MIDI, audio effects, built-in synths, and more.

  • MIDI: Import, record and edit MIDI directly on the timeline, play using a MIDI controller or your typing keyboard.
  • Chat bar: Generate instruments and vocals, create new and totally unique plugins and synth presets, and even ask for help.
  • Audio effects: Dial in your mix with high-quality audio effects, including sidechain compression and convolution reverb. Or design your own using the chat bar.
  • Wavetable synth: Powerful and versatile synth for adding basses, leads, pads, chords and other synth sounds to your songs. Design your own presets using the chat bar.
  • Musical typing: Play MIDI parts and find fresh inspiration using your typing keyboard, featuring an arpeggiator and chord mode.
  • Automation: Change your effect parameters over time to breathe life into your final mix.

Sounds is a new Create mode that lets you create individual one-shot samples and loops from scratch.

Meet our new Create features: Sample + Mashup

We just dropped two new features: Mashup and Sample, which give you a ton of options when you want to take your songs to new places.

Mashup lets you combine any two songs, mash them together and come up with something totally unique. Try blending two similar tracks to make a new version that borrows elements from both. Or choose two totally different tracks to get happy accidents beyond your wildest imagination.

Replace Section

Oct 10, 2024

Pro and Premier users can now Replace Sections of songs.

What You Can Do

1 - Change Lyrics: Perfect for tweaking the words in your song.

2 - Add Instrumental Breaks: insert an instrumental section like a guitar riff or a drum break.

How to Use It

1 - Access the Feature: Right-click on a song or click the vertical “...” to open the More Actions menu.

2 - Navigate to Replace Section: Go to Edit > Replace Section.

3 - Enter Edit Mode: You’ll be taken into Edit Mode with the song. Select the song portion you wish to recreate (must be 10-30 seconds long). Lyrics (if present) will automatically be populated into the Lyrics box.

4 - Update your Lyrics (if you wish): Update your lyrics or try typing [drum break]

Recreate the Section: Click the “Replace Section” button.

5 - Choose Between Sections: Two versions will be generated. Listen to both and click “Select” on the one you prefer.

6 - Select a Section: Once you select a section, you’ll be redirected to the Create page where a full song will begin generating automatically.

Crop Songs

Sep 24, 2024

Pro and Premier users can now Crop Songs on Suno. Cropping allows you to keep a portion of your song and remove everything else.

How to use:

1 - Find a song you’ve created in your Library or in Create

2 - Right click or go into the More Actions menu

3 - Hover over “Edit” and click “Crop Song”

4 - Use the pink waveform selectors to pick the start / end of portion of your song you would like to keep

5 - Click “Crop Song”

6 - A new song will be added to Create and your Library

Also:

You can now Edit Displayed Lyrics from directly within Edit > Song Details.

Currently Crop Songs and the new Edit Details workflows are desktop-only. We are working to port these to tablet-web and mobile-web.

Covers (in early-access beta) is available to all Pro/Premier subscribers. Covers allow you to keep the melody of your songs but adopt them into a different style.

V3 Alpha Access for Pro & Premier Members

Feb 22, 2024

V3 is coming soon, and we believe it has the potential to redefine the state-of-the-art for generative music. We need your help testing our new model before we release it to the full community, so we're sharing access to an early version of V3, V3 Alpha, with you and other Pro and Premier members.

V3 Alpha features

V3 Alpha has better audio quality and increased expressiveness among other heavily requested features:

⏲️ Extended max clip length of two minutes

🚀 Faster song generation

🎸 Dedicated instrumental support

🌍 Expanded language support

➡️ Continue from anywhere (backward compatible with songs you made with V2)

How to use V3 Alpha

We think V3 Alpha creates more realistic, authentic music, but we want to know what you think! Follow these steps to test V3 Alpha:

1. Go to https://app.suno.ai/create/

2. Change V2 to V3 Alpha. You can go back to using V2 anytime during the alpha access period by switching back to V2 from V3 Alpha.

Please like (👍) and dislike (👎) songs that you like and dislike. This is the fastest, most direct way to help us improve V3, but you can also share feedback with @suno_ai_ on X.

A word of caution

V3 is still under development, and the early version we're sharing today still has weaknesses. V3 Alpha struggles to follow certain prompts like key signatures and BPM, and its songs don't always sound perfectly mixed and mastered. By popular demand, V3 Alpha can also make longer songs, but it can also be prone to hallucination — especially when fed short prompts.

You may need to prompt V3 Alpha differently than V2 for best results, and V3 Alpha will change without notice as we continue development and work toward a full release by making improvements in these areas. We look forward to hearing your feedback, and we hope you enjoy V3 Alpha!

Deleting Songs

You can now delete songs using the Move to Trash button. To delete a song:

To delete a song permanently, select the song in your Trash, and click `Delete Permanently`.
