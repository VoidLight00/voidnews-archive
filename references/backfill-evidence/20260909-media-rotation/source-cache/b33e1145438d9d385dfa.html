What's New on Tripo | August

August was a busy month at Tripo. We shipped a new model, rebuilt key parts of the community experience, and made export workflows smoother.

Spotlights

Tripo New Model - P2.0 Preview

Tripo P2.0 Preview is the industry's first AI model to generate native quad topology from multi-view input. It creates cleaner edge flow and more structured meshes, often in 10 to 40 seconds. It supports up to 50K triangles or 25K quads, covering most hero characters and props in game pipelines. Semantic segmentation also splits meshes along natural boundaries, so clothing, armor, and other parts can be handled separately for rigging or texturing.

👉 Try P2.0 Preview

Community Spotlight: Rebuilt Gallery & Instant AI Previews

Showcase your talent in our upgraded Gallery! Click "Feature My Model," choose your top asset, and use free AI renders to generate stunning previews and descriptions in seconds. Featured models earn 10 credits and get shared to Discord. Plus, stand out with subscriber-exclusive custom avatar frames on your community profile!

👉 Explore the Gallery & Feature Your Model

Workflow Upgrades

DCC Plugin Updates: Blender, Maya, 3ds Max, Unreal

The round trip between Tripo and your DCC tools got smoother this month. Blender Bridge v1.0.3 stays connected more reliably. Maya and 3ds Max Bridge v1.0.1 give you more control over cache location. Unreal API v1.0.1 has been upgraded to API V3 with a redesigned panel, adding support for H3.1 models and P1.0 quad meshes for game assets.

3D Tools: Viewer, Compressor, and More

The 3D Viewer now supports 20+ file formats. We also launched a browser-based File Compressor, so you can shrink models and compare before and after without installing anything.

👉 Open 3D Viewer

FBX Exports Are Faster and More Reliable

If you export FBX regularly, you should notice the difference. Dense meshes with 60K+ faces now parse up to 80% faster. We also fixed black patches on some normal maps and corrected face orientation on export, which means less cleanup after import.

More Ways to Use Tripo

Vietnamese Language Support

The homepage is now available in Vietnamese.

Discord Account Linking

You can now connect your Discord account in settings. That makes identity linking across platforms easier, and featured models can auto-post to the Discord showcase.
