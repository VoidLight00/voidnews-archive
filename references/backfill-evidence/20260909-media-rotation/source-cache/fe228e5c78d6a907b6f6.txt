Ethan Tandowsky has joined ElevenLabs as chief financial officer.
Ethan was most recently CFO of Adyen, the payments company that provides critical infrastructure to many of the world's largest enterprises. He joined Adyen's finance team in 2016, and helped take the company public in 2018, before becoming CFO in 2023. During his time there, Adyen grew from 400 to 5,000 people, expanded into new international markets, and increased revenue 20x.
ElevenLabs is entering a new stage of scale. We are now a global team of 800 people, serving millions of developers, creators, and businesses. Our industry-leading voice and audio AI models support over 90 languages, and we have launched 10 million conversational agents across sales, support, hiring, training, and many new product experiences. Large organizations now account for 55% of our revenue (up from 40% this time last year), and we’re proud to serve enterprises like Nvidia, Stripe, Klarna, and Deutsche Telekom, and governments from Brazil to Ukraine. At the same time, we are building new financial models to support the creative ecosystem, including the industry’s first marketplace for creators to earn money from their voice IP.
As the business becomes more complex, Ethan will scale our financial infrastructure and shape how we invest in research, product, and global expansion to support our long-term growth.
“ElevenLabs has the research depth and customer demand to meaningfully improve how people interact day-to-day with AI,” said Ethan Tandowsky, CFO of ElevenLabs. “My focus is to drive investment and build the financial foundation that allows the company to seize the significant opportunity ahead. I'm excited by the opportunity to work with this incredible team to further scale ElevenLabs globally, and deliver AI that feels native and authentic in every context.”
“ElevenLabs started as an audio research lab and we now have a broad platform for businesses deploying AI that interacts naturally with people,” said Mati Staniszewski, cofounder and CEO of ElevenLabs. “Ethan brings a strong track record of scaling financial operations in high-growth environments, and valuable experience as CFO of a public company. I look forward to working with him to solve human-AI interaction for more businesses around the world.”
