Prerequisites Before you begin, verify you have: Administrative access to the Microsoft Entra admin center, the Microsoft identity and access management service (formerly Azure AD). Application Developer permissions (or higher) in your Microsoft tenant. Access to Amazon Quick in your AWS account. Familiarity with OAuth 2.0 authentication flows. OAuth 2.0 is the industry-standard protocol that Quick uses to securely access Outlook on your behalf without storing your password. You grant specific permissions, such as reading email or managing calendars, rather than sharing credentials. Walkthrough This integration uses Microsoft Graph API to connect Outlook with Amazon Quick through OAuth 2.0 authentication. Configure your Outlook connector by completing the following steps. Step 1: Add the Microsoft Outlook connector In the Amazon Quick navigation menu, choose Connect apps and data , and then choose Connectors . Search for Outlook , and then choose Connect . Figure 1: Adding the Microsoft Outlook connector in Amazon Quick Step 2: Authorize the integration A sign-in page appears for you to sign in with your Outlook email. If you signed in previously with different email addresses, choose the one you want to use. For first-time configuration, choose Add account or Use another account , and then sign in with your email address and password. Figure 2: Signing in with your Outlook account Step 3: Authorize Amazon Quick to access your Outlook email After you sign in to Outlook, choose your current Amazon Quick account to authorize Amazon Quick to access your Microsoft Outlook email. Figure 3: Authorizing Amazon Quick to access Outlook Step 4: Check the connection status After you authorize Outlook, the connector displays a Signed in , Ready status. (Optional) To test the connection, choose Try it . Figure 4: Confirming the connection status Step 5 (optional): Test Outlook email actions Choose one of the sample questions, such as checking email, writing an email, or summarizing calendar events. Figure 5: Testing Outlook email actions with sample questions Use cases and benefits With Microsoft Outlook integrated into Amazon Quick, you can address communication and workflow challenges across several categories. The following use cases are grouped by Quick tool, each with the customer outcomes you can expect. Quick chat agent use cases Customer support email assistance: Support teams can use Amazon Quick chat agents to draft professional, consistent responses to customer inquiries. The chat agent accesses your knowledge base, product documentation, and previous case resolutions to suggest contextually appropriate replies. This can help reduce average response time while maintaining quality standards. Executive communication support: Executive assistants and leadership teams find chat agents valuable for summarizing lengthy email threads and identifying critical action items. The chat agents can also draft communications that match the appropriate tone and formality level for different audiences. This can help executives spend less time on email management and correspondence. Sales team enablement: Sales representatives can query chat agents directly from Outlook to retrieve pricing information, competitive positioning, and customer history. They can then incorporate these insights into prospect communications without switching applications or searching through multiple systems. Quick Flows use cases Automated meeting briefings: Configure Quick Flows to run before scheduled meetings, automatically pulling relevant email, documents, and context into a briefing summary delivered to your inbox. Teams might experience time savings in meeting preparation. Weekly status report generation: Scheduled flows can aggregate email communications, extract project updates, and compile status reports. This can help reduce the time spent on manual report compilation per team member. Action item extraction and task creation: Build flows that monitor incoming email, identify action items and commitments, and automatically create tasks in project management tools such as Asana or Monday.com. This can help reduce missed deadlines through automated task tracking. Quick Automate use cases Customer onboarding orchestration: Automate the end-to-end customer onboarding process. When a new customer signs their contract (detected through email), Quick Automate triggers account provisioning, schedules kickoff meetings, sends welcome communications, and notifies internal stakeholders. This can help accelerate onboarding while reducing manual coordination errors. Cross-departmental coordination: Orchestrate complex processes that span departments, such as employee onboarding that coordinates between HR, IT, facilities, and the hiring manager. Email serves as both a notification channel and a trigger for subsequent workflow steps. This can help accelerate new hire setup through automated cross-department coordination.
